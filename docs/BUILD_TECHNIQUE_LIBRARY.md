# Build Technique Library

Technique cards live in `packs/vanilla/techniques/`. They are reusable design operations rather than aesthetic labels.

Recommended order:

1. structural hierarchy
2. player scale
3. depth layering / wall bays / facade depth
4. support logic and transition detail
5. controlled gradients and material story
6. sightline control and focal composition
7. negative space
8. repetition breaking and asymmetry
9. lighting hierarchy
10. contextual clutter

Do not apply every technique everywhere. The point of the library is to choose the right operation for the current problem.

# Maturity States

- **CONCEPT** — visual or written direction only.
- **BLOCKOUT** — massing and rough room placement.
- **ARCHITECTURE** — structure, circulation, connectors, scale.
- **DETAILING** — materials, trim, furniture, decor, vegetation, lighting.
- **REVIEW_CANDIDATE** — ready for focused in-game review.
- **APPROVED** — user-approved candidate for its current scope.
- **MERGED** — approved module integrated into a larger master.

Approval belongs to a specific scope. A ceiling can be APPROVED while the surrounding room remains DETAILING.

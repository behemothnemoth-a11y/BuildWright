# Density and Negative Space

The most common microblock failure is over-detailing.

## Density zoning
- **Quiet:** broad wall, floor, ceiling field. TIER 0–1.
- **Structural:** arches, piers, railings, trusses. TIER 1–2.
- **Human:** furniture, handles, frames, consoles. TIER 2–3.
- **Focal:** crest, statue, chandelier, hero portal. TIER 3–4.

Do not spread focal density evenly around a room. Detail should cluster around use, touch, craftsmanship and sightline focal points.

## Ceiling rule
Large ceilings are especially vulnerable to visual noise. Preserve a few strong trusses/ribs and large quiet infill fields. Microblock joinery belongs at structural intersections, not every rafter.

# Microblock QA Checklist

## Compare to vanilla
- Does the original architectural hierarchy still read?
- Did any opening, stair, landing, corridor or connector shrink?
- Did a quiet surface become noisy for no reason?

## Geometry
- No stray disconnected cells.
- No accidental paper-thin collision traps in circulation.
- Adjacent-host seams align.
- Repeated assets vary without losing family identity.

## Materials
- Provider supports every cell material/state.
- Directional state transforms correctly.
- Functional source blocks kept vanilla when behavior matters.

## Transform
- 90/180/270 rotation behaves.
- Mirror behaves.
- Save/reload does not double-transform.

## In-game
- Player-eye view reads better than vanilla.
- Distant silhouette still reads.
- Lighting does not expose ugly micro seams.
- Dense areas remain navigable and visually calm enough.

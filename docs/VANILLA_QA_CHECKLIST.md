# Vanilla QA Checklist

## Architecture
- primary silhouette reads immediately
- supports appear plausible
- connectors/circulation remain open
- walls/facades have intentional depth
- roof system can be explained in one sentence

## Player scale
- corridors and doors clear
- furniture usable
- railings and landings safe/readable
- detail concentration works from eye level

## Detail
- no barcode repetition
- gradients are spatially logical
- negative space survives
- clutter has function/story
- focal points dominate nearby noise

## Vegetation
- repeated plants vary
- roots/trunks feel grounded
- landscaping supports architecture

## Lighting
- circulation visible
- focal lighting intentional
- fixtures not over-scaled
- dark zones are deliberate, not accidental

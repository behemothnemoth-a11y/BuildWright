# Variation & Anti-Repetition

When repeating an asset family, vary at least two of:

- dimensions;
- rotation/mirror;
- palette role balance;
- wear;
- clutter sockets;
- foliage density;
- local trim;
- adjacent negative space.

Do not alternate variants mechanically A-B-A-B. Variation should follow context: corners, focal bays, circulation, exposure, room function and age.

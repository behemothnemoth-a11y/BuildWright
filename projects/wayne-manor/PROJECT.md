# Wayne Manor + Batcave

Buildwright seed project.

## Direction

Massive Gothic Wayne Manor with player-scale circulation, dense but controlled high-end vanilla detailing, then a separate microblock enhancement pass.

## Locked project rules

- Grand stair flights are functional circulation and must connect to future rooms/corridors.
- Major rooms can be monumental, but doors, landings, furniture and movement remain player scale.
- Do not solve weak architecture by adding repeated ornament.
- Roof structure should use large readable bays and negative space rather than excessive repeated ribs.
- Vanilla master remains independently usable before microblock conversion.

## Whole-building graph

BuildWright 0.7 adds an offline whole-building regression graph at `project_graph/phase_a.json`. It is intentionally not project canon yet. It exists to test room attachment, corridors, exposed facade detection, roof massing, and site assembly while the hand-refined Grand Hall remains authoritative.

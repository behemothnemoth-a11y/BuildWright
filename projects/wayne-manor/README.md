# Wayne Manor + Batcave

First full BuildWright project.

## Direction

Massive Gothic Wayne Manor with player-scale interiors and a deeply integrated Batcave.

The architectural target is monumental but readable from player height. Detail should follow the BuildWright vanilla hierarchy rather than relying on sheer density.

## Grand Hall

Key requirements:

- monumental Gothic first impression
- genuine high roof volume
- restrained, readable hammerbeam structure
- split ceremonial stairs
- both upper stair landings continue into real future rooms/corridors
- central circulation under/around balcony remains usable
- deep Gothic wall bays
- fireplaces, galleries, furniture, portraits, armor, books, plants, and lighting at player scale

## Editions

- Vanilla master first
- Microblock edition later
